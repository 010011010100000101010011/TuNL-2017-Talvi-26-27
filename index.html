6-Month Progressive Physical Training Plan — U10, Indoor

Built from the FIFA Training Centre series “Athleticism for young players” (Tony Colbert): dynamic mobility, core strength, functional lower-body strength and explosivity — high-value bodyweight work with minimal equipment. Monday 60 min + Saturday 90 min, 24 weeks, 4 phases. Every session has a detailed page an assistant coach can pick up and run.

Non-negotiables (from the sources — why the plan looks like this)

1 · Smart training: more from less

Only high-value, multi-dimensional exercises. No one-dimensional filler, no sapping pre-fatigue. Athletic work complements football time, it never eats it. Bodyweight only; “load” = range, one leg, instability, rotation, speed — never heavy weight.

2 · Order of the pillars

Mobility is the bedrock → core is the fulcrum → lower-body strength makes force → explosivity turns force into speed. The plan follows that order across the phases and within each session.

3 · Power on a fresh system

Jumps/velocity work = 3–4 reps, long rests, maximal intent, early in the session, never after a tired drill. Loud landing = too high, too many, or not ready. Phase 1 has zero jumping: landing school first.

4 · Progress on quality, not load

Gate before every progression: 6 clean reps full range · neutral spine & level hips · quiet controlled landings. Then progress range → support → rotation → height → speed/partner.

5 · Weekly dose (FIFA)

Every week: 2× daily microdose mobility (warm-up + cool-down), 1× core development block (Mon), 1× lower-body strength block (Sat), 1× power microdose (Sat, P2+). That's exactly the recommended exposure — nothing extra.

6 · 10 balls → split groups

Ball station = max 10 players (one ball each). Physical station = 0 balls. Play/competition = 1–2 balls with touch limits. 15+ players → third group is always play/competition/fun.

The four phases

Weekly rhythm

Monday · 60 min — CORE day

MinWhat
0–10Whole group: dynamic mobility warm-up (phase set)
10–30 / 32–52Two 20-min blocks, groups swap: physical core circuit ↔ ball station (theme/placeholder) (+ play group if 15+)
52–60Cool-down mobility (slow re-run = post-session development dose) + debrief

Saturday · 90 min — LEGS + POWER day

MinWhat
0–10Whole group: mobility warm-up + 2-min ladder/hurdle flash
10–20Whole group: power microdose (fresh system). P1 = landing school & speed games
20–80Three 18-min station rotations: physical (legs) ↔ ball station ↔ play/competition (2 groups if ≤14: physical ↔ ball, longer blocks)
80–90Whole group final game 6 min + cool-down/debrief 4 min

The hall (40 × 30 m, curtains → 3 zones)

Zone 1 — Physical stationMats (landings + cool-down), 2 jump boxes, 2 step ladders, 6 hurdles, hanging ropes. 0 balls.
Zone 2 — Ball stationUp to 10 players, 1 ball each + 6 small goals. Head coach's theme slot / placeholder drills.
Zone 3 — Play / competition1–2 balls, rondos, box games, relays, obstacle-course fun. This is the 3rd-group activity when 15+.

— curtains divide the 40×30 m hall into three ~13×30 m areas —

Equipment: 10 balls · 2 step ladders · 6 small hurdles · 2 jump boxes · hanging ropes · 3 large mattresses · 6 small goals · optional “resistance” = filled backpack or partner's hand (only once bodyweight is perfect). Rope uses: assisted pistol squats & step-downs (P2+), hang/grip circuit, tug & tow games in play zone. Mattresses: always around/in front of box jumps; stack for glute-ham shoulders-on-box and safe play landings.
